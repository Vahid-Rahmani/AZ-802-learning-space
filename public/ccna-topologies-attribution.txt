CCNA topology library — source and attribution
==============================================

What this library is
--------------------
The /ccna page section "Every lab topology, level by level" is a raw, read-only index of 101 CCNA
200-301 lab topologies, grouped into six levels that follow the six official 200-301 v1.1 domains in
blueprint order. Each entry shows a catalog number, the lab name, the catalog's own short summary and,
where one exists, the topology diagram (96 of 101 labs).

Where the material comes from
-----------------------------
  Product    : NetSim 200-301 CCNA (product code CIS-NS-200301-01-02)
  Catalog    : https://api2.netsim.boson.com (the public guest lab catalog behind
               https://netsim.boson.com/labs)
  Retrieved  : 2026-10-08
  Endpoints  : /users/labtree, /labs/{id}/labId, /labs/{id}/topology-image

Lab names, summaries and topology diagrams are the published catalog metadata and the published
diagram images of Boson NetSim, a commercial product of Boson Software, LLC. They were not written or
drawn by this project, they are not covered by this project's license, and they are shown here
unedited as a personal study index so that a learner can recognize a topology before building it.

What is not included
--------------------
The licensed endpoint paths that carry the product's own lab instructions, step-by-step text and
objective metadata (/labs/{id}/document, /labs/{id}/meta, /labs/{id}/topology) require a paid NetSim
seat and answer HTTP 401 to unauthenticated requests. None of that material was retrieved, and none of
it is published here. No Cisco exam questions, dumps or paid-course content are part of this library.
